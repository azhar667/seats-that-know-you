01 / PERSON + SEAT
LIGHT · SCREEN · SOUND
01 / The idea

One cabin. Different people.
Personal experiences.

Cars are shared by many, but personalization is usually designed around one person. We connect the passenger with the seat they occupy.

01 — RECOGNITION

Who sits where

An existing interior camera recognizes a passenger and their seat. Unknown passengers enter as guests.

02 — MY SEAT

A personal zone

Each passenger gets their own light identity, screen experience, sound zone and available comfort settings.

03 — MOVES WITH YOU

The experience follows

When a person changes seats or vehicles, their profile and preferences can follow them. Guest data is not saved.

02 / Why it matters

A shared space has
individual needs.

The project began with a simple tension: manual control asks too much, while whole-car control changes too much.

12×Rear-seat children can be more distracting than a phone, according to the cited Monash University research.
69%DTS (2022): drivers who consider contextual personalization important.
92%Blue Yonder (2024): respondents willing to pay extra for personalization.
THE PROBLEM

People share the car.
Controls stay in one place.

A passenger may need a different temperature, sound level or screen experience — but changing one central setting can affect everyone.

THE DESIGN OPPORTUNITY

Change only the zone
that needs it.

The system links who is present with where they sit, then responds through light, screens and sound without disturbing the rest of the cabin.

03 / Passenger experience

From arrival to the ride.

The latest presentation describes the system as a set of connected interactions, not a single recognition moment.

01 / ARRIVE

03 / GUEST

DESIGN DECISIONS

What changed

Fingerprint sensor → existing camera · rainbow-like colors → Champagne + mood-light family · child-centred story → a system for all passengers · decorative glow → calmer, readable screens.

LIGHT IDENTITY

Champagne + eight seat colors

One person keeps one color across zones. Color is never the only signal: position, name and motion also communicate state.

05 / Prototype & principles

Test the experience, not just the idea.

The camera prototype explores recognition, seat assignment and sleep/wake interaction in a controlled setup.

CAMERA V2

Two-seat recognition

Left and right passengers are assigned to their corresponding screens; the display follows when they change seats.

TEST RESULT

8 of 9 attempts

The earlier camera test correctly recognized 8 of 9 attempts. In the failed attempt, the system showed Guest rather than a wrong name.

PRIVACY

Local processing

The project direction describes in-car processing, no stored video, and no saved guest profile. Consent and deletion remain important.
